# Shootplan PARES

Stijl: lookbook op film, 90s retro voetbal. Zwart-wit als basis, wijnrood (bordeaux) als enige kleuraccent in kleding of details. Niemand poseert, behalve bij een bewust portret. Geen merklogo's van anderen in beeld.

Voor nu maken we deze beelden met AI. Later dezelfde lijst gebruiken voor een echte shoot of voetbaldag (camera: analoog of een digitale camera met een Portra-preset).

## Prompts (Engels, werken in ChatGPT image, Gemini, Flux, Artlist)

Avoid-lijst voor elk beeld: no centered symmetrical composition, no one looking at the camera, no HDR, no oversaturation, no plastic clean look, no front-on eye-level view, no visible brand logos, no text.

### 1. Pleintje, gouden uur

Candid documentary photograph of kids playing street football on a worn concrete square between Dutch apartment blocks, late afternoon. Shot from across the square with a 135mm telephoto, compressed perspective, photographer unnoticed at a distance. Low camera position, a blurred metal fence in the foreground partly framing the scene. Golden-hour backlight, dust kicked up behind the ball, long shadows. One boy in a plain burgundy shirt caught mid-kick with slight motion blur, the others reacting, nobody looking at the camera. Shot on Kodak Portra 400, visible grain, soft halation, muted warm tones, slightly underexposed shadows. Off-center composition. 3:2.

### 2. Kooi bij nacht

Night photograph inside a fenced street football cage in a Dutch neighbourhood, shot with a cheap disposable camera with hard direct flash. Teenagers mid-game, one in a burgundy jersey turning with the ball, motion blur in the background, the chain-link fence lit harshly by the flash and the sky pitch black behind it. Shot from just outside the fence, lens close to the wire so it softly blurs in the foreground. Fuji Superia 800 film, heavy grain, flash falloff, slight red-eye glow on reflective surfaces. Raw, 90s snapshot feel. 4:5.

### 3. Twee jassen als doel

Quiet still-life photograph of two jackets lying on a patch of worn grass as makeshift goalposts, a scuffed football resting between them, in a Dutch park on an overcast morning. Shot low to the ground with a 35mm lens at f/4, grass blades out of focus in the foreground. Black-and-white, Ilford HP5 film, deep blacks, visible grain, soft even overcast light. Plenty of negative space above. 3:2.

### 4. Kleedkamer jaren 90

Documentary photograph of an empty amateur football club changing room in the Netherlands, 1990s feel. Burgundy and navy shirts hanging on wooden hooks, worn wooden bench, muddy boots underneath, tiled wall. Soft window light from the left, dust in the air. Shot from the doorway with a 35mm lens, slightly tilted, as if someone just walked in. Kodak Gold 200, warm faded colors, grain, slight vignette. 4:5.

### 5. Portret met bal

Environmental portrait of a 12-year-old girl holding a football under her arm, leaning against a green metal fence at the edge of a neighbourhood football pitch. She looks straight into the lens, calm and confident. 85mm lens at f/2, background blocks of flats softly blurred. Overcast daylight. Plain burgundy training top. Kodak Portra 400, natural skin tones, fine grain, muted grade. 4:5.

### 6. Voeten op kunstgras

Close-up photograph at ground level of a kid's feet in worn old football boots next to a football on artificial turf, black rubber crumbs scattered around. Hard low side light from the setting sun, long shadows across the turf fibres. Macro-ish 50mm lens at f/2.8, shallow depth of field. Black-and-white, high contrast, Kodak Tri-X grain. 16:9.

## Later: echte shoot

- Eén middag op een Cruyff Court of pleintje, liefst tijdens de eerste PARES-voetbaldag.
- Altijd toestemming van ouders voor foto's van kinderen, schriftelijk.
- Gouden uur (anderhalf uur voor zonsondergang) en één ronde na donker met flits.
- Shirts in effen bordeaux en wit meenemen, zodat de kleuren kloppen zonder logo's van andere merken.
