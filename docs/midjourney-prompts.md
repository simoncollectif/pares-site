# Midjourney-prompts PARES

Voor de beelden die nog ontbreken. Zelfde opbouw als Simons eigen prompts: SUBJECT, CONTEXT, STYLE, CAMERA/LIGHTING. Achter elke prompt staan de parameters. Geen merklogo's, want de beelden komen op de site.

Al gekozen: schaduwen onder het viaduct, de favela-trap, de straat in Havana en Simons eigen favela-uitzicht.

## 1. Retro shirts, editorial (het moderne stuk)

A candid editorial photograph of four friends in their early twenties hanging out at a worn neighbourhood football cage after a game. SUBJECT: They wear real vintage 1990s football shirts with bold geometric collars and shadow-stripe patterns in burgundy, navy and faded white, no sponsors and no brand logos, with baggy track pants and beaten-up sneakers. One sits on the ball, one leans on the fence, two laugh at something off-frame. Nobody poses for the camera. CONTEXT: Chain-link fence, cracked asphalt with puddles, a block of flats behind, grey sky. STYLE: 1990s football fanzine meets modern streetwear lookbook, shot on Kodak Portra 800 with direct flash, real skin, sweat, slightly overexposed foreground, unretouched. CAMERA/LIGHTING: 28mm, shot from low and close as if the photographer is one of the friends, horizon slightly tilted. --ar 4:5 --style raw --v 7 --no logos, text, sponsors, posing, studio

## 2. Pleintje in Nederland

A black and white documentary photograph of a Dutch neighbourhood football square on a grey Wednesday afternoon. SUBJECT: Kids in a loose game, one goalkeeper diving on the tiles, the others a blur of movement, schoolbags piled against the fence. Faces turned away or blurred. CONTEXT: A small fenced pitch between brick 1960s apartment blocks, bikes parked against the fence, a mother with a pram walking past. STYLE: Raw Dutch street photography in the style of 1980s documentary books, Ilford HP5 pushed, coarse grain, deep blacks. CAMERA/LIGHTING: Shot through the fence from outside, 35mm, the wire softly blurred in the foreground, flat overcast light. --ar 3:2 --style raw --v 7 --no logos, text

## 3. Kooi bij nacht

A night photograph of teenagers playing in a fenced street football cage. SUBJECT: One player shielding the ball with his back to the camera, the others half lost in darkness. CONTEXT: A single orange street lamp, wet asphalt reflecting the light, flats with a few lit windows behind. STYLE: Raw 1990s snapshot on Fuji Superia 800 with harsh on-camera flash, flash falloff into black, heavy grain, slight motion blur. CAMERA/LIGHTING: Shot from the corner of the cage at knee height, 28mm, flash plus available light. --ar 4:5 --style raw --v 7 --no logos, text, faces looking at camera

## 4. Várzea, Brazilië

A wide colour photograph of an amateur várzea football match on a dusty red-earth pitch on the edge of São Paulo. SUBJECT: Players in mismatched faded shirts, spectators on a low concrete wall and on the roof of a parked car, a dog crossing the pitch. CONTEXT: Self-built brick houses climbing the hill behind, a hand-painted goal, power lines. STYLE: Faded colour film, Kodak Gold 200, warm dust in the air, documentary, nobody aware of the camera. CAMERA/LIGHTING: Shot from a rooftop at the side of the pitch, 35mm, late afternoon haze. --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 7 --no logos, text

## 5. Detail: oude bal en krijt

A close black and white photograph of a scuffed old leather football resting against a chalk-drawn goal on a brick wall. SUBJECT: The ball and the rough chalk lines, a kid's hand just entering the frame. CONTEXT: A narrow alley, worn bricks, a puddle. STYLE: Fine art documentary, high contrast, grain. CAMERA/LIGHTING: 50mm at f/2, hard low side light. --ar 3:2 --style raw --v 7 --no logos, text

## 6. Meisjes die voetballen

A black and white candid photograph of a girls' football game on a concrete court in an inner-city neighbourhood. SUBJECT: A girl with braids caught mid-sprint with the ball, two defenders chasing, all in mismatched training tops. Faces partly turned away, joy in the body language. CONTEXT: Graffiti on a low wall, a basketball hoop without a net, flats behind. STYLE: Raw documentary, Tri-X, grain, slight motion blur, real. CAMERA/LIGHTING: 85mm from the side of the court, panning with the runner, overcast. --ar 3:2 --style raw --v 7 --no logos, text

# Tenues (oktober 2026)

Voor de vijf stukken in de rubriek Tenues. Geen gezichten: alleen kleding, details, ruggen en plekken. Geen merklogo's of sponsors, want de beelden komen op de site. Liggend 3:2 voor het grote beeld bovenaan een stuk, staand 4:5 als alternatief voor Instagram.

## 1. Het trainingsjack is terug

A documentary still life of a vintage 1980s football track jacket, deep burgundy with thin white piping down the sleeves and a stand-up collar, no logos, hanging from the chain-link fence of an empty neighbourhood football cage at dusk. SUBJECT: The jacket alone, zipper half open, slightly creased, one sleeve moving in the wind. CONTEXT: Wet asphalt, a single street lamp just switching on, blocks of flats out of focus behind the fence. STYLE: Raw editorial streetwear photography meets 1990s football fanzine, Kodak Portra 800, visible grain, muted colours, unretouched. CAMERA/LIGHTING: 50mm at f/2, shot from a low angle through the fence, blue hour with warm sodium light on the fabric. --ar 3:2 --style raw --v 7 --no faces, people, logos, text, brand marks

Alternatief: A young man seen only from behind walking off a floodlit concrete pitch at night, wearing a burgundy vintage track jacket with white piping and loose navy track pants, a football under his arm, no logos. Harsh on-camera flash, Fuji Superia 800, grain, slight motion blur, 28mm from behind. --ar 4:5 --style raw --v 7 --no face, logos, text

## 2. Retro voetbaltruitjes in Amsterdam

A documentary photograph of a rail of vintage 1990s football shirts in a small Amsterdam vintage shop. SUBJECT: Tightly packed shirts on wooden hangers, bold geometric 90s patterns, shadow stripes and big collars in orange, burgundy, navy and white, no club crests, no sponsors, no brand logos. CONTEXT: Soft daylight from a tall canal-house window, old wooden floorboards, a hand-written price tag on string. STYLE: Editorial shop photography, Kodak Gold 200, warm faded colours, grain, slightly imperfect framing. CAMERA/LIGHTING: 35mm at f/2.8, shot along the rail so the shirts recede into soft focus, window light from the side. --ar 3:2 --style raw --v 7 --no people, faces, logos, text, crests, sponsors

## 3. Het felste Oranje-shirt ooit

A bold still life of a plain, extremely bright orange football shirt with no crest, no logos and no sponsor, draped over the railing of a Dutch apartment balcony, with black football shorts folded beside it. SUBJECT: The shirt glowing against grey brick and a grey sky, fabric moving slightly. CONTEXT: A typical 1960s Dutch block of flats, laundry rack and a flower pot just in frame, overcast late afternoon. STYLE: Graphic editorial photography, colour film with the orange as the only saturated colour, grain, clean and minimal. CAMERA/LIGHTING: 85mm from the street below looking up, flat overcast light. --ar 3:2 --style raw --v 7 --no people, faces, logos, crests, text, sponsors

## 4. De lelijkste voetbalshirts van dit seizoen

An overhead flat-lay photograph of a chaotic pile of loud 1990s-style football and goalkeeper shirts on a worn formica table in an amateur football club canteen. SUBJECT: Clashing wild patterns, zigzags, paint splashes, neon green, purple, teal and pink, no logos, no crests, no sponsors. CONTEXT: A half-empty coffee cup, a plastic tray, beer mats and a whistle at the edges of the table. STYLE: Playful editorial still life, direct on-camera flash, saturated colours, film grain, slightly harsh shadows. CAMERA/LIGHTING: Shot straight down from above, 35mm, flash. --ar 3:2 --style raw --v 7 --no people, faces, hands, logos, text, crests

## 5. Van blokecore naar cottagecore

A quiet interior photograph of an old British-style living room where a floral-print football shirt is folded on a worn velvet armchair. SUBJECT: The shirt with a soft rose print and a lace-like collar detail, a knitted club scarf over the armrest, a cup of tea on a doily. CONTEXT: Patterned wallpaper, a small old television in the corner showing a blurred green football pitch, net curtains, late afternoon light. STYLE: Cottagecore meets football fanzine, Kodak Portra 400, soft warm tones, fine grain, nostalgic and calm. CAMERA/LIGHTING: 50mm at f/2 from standing height, window light through net curtains. --ar 3:2 --style raw --v 7 --no people, faces, logos, text, crests

Alternatief (4:5): Close-up of hands-free details only: a velvet track top in bottle green with a crocheted stripe down the sleeve, lying on a floral wallpaper-patterned sofa cushion next to an old football programme, warm window light, Portra 400, macro-ish 50mm. --ar 4:5 --style raw --v 7 --no people, faces, logos, text
