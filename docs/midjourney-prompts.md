# Midjourney-prompts PARES

Voor de beelden die nog ontbreken. Zelfde opbouw als Simons eigen prompts: SUBJECT, CONTEXT, STYLE, CAMERA/LIGHTING. Achter elke prompt staan de parameters. Geen merklogo's, want de beelden komen op de site.

Al gekozen: schaduwen onder het viaduct, de favela-trap, de straat in Havana en Simons eigen favela-uitzicht.

## 1. Retro shirts, editorial (het moderne stuk)

A candid editorial photograph of four friends in their early twenties hanging out at a worn neighbourhood football cage after a game. SUBJECT: They wear real vintage 1990s football shirts with bold geometric collars and shadow-stripe patterns in burgundy, navy and faded white, no sponsors and no brand logos, with baggy track pants and beaten-up sneakers. One sits on the ball, one leans on the fence, two laugh at something off-frame. Nobody poses for the camera. CONTEXT: Chain-link fence, cracked asphalt with puddles, a block of flats behind, grey sky. STYLE: 1990s football fanzine meets modern streetwear lookbook, shot on Kodak Portra 800 with direct flash, real skin, sweat, slightly overexposed foreground, unretouched. CAMERA/LIGHTING: 28mm, shot from low and close as if the photographer is one of the friends, horizon slightly tilted. --ar 4:5 --style raw --v 7 --no logos, text, sponsors, posing, studio

## 2. Pleintje in Nederland

A black and white documentary photograph of a Dutch neighbourhood football square on a grey Wednesday afternoon. SUBJECT: Kids in a loose game, one goalkeeper diving on the tiles, the others a blur of movement, schoolbags piled against the fence. Faces turned away or blurred. CONTEXT: A small fenced pitch between brick 1960s apartment blocks, bikes parked against the fence, a mother with a pram walking past. STYLE: Raw Dutch street photography in the style of 1980s documentary books, Ilford HP5 pushed, coarse grain, deep blacks. CAMERA/LIGHTING: Shot through the fence from outside, 35mm, the wire softly blurred in the foreground, flat overcast light. --ar 3:2 --style raw --v 7 --no logos, text

## 3. Kooi bij nacht

A night photograph of teenagers playing in a fenced street football cage. SUBJECT: One player shielding the ball with his back to the camera, the others half lost in darkness. CONTEXT: A single orange street lamp, wet asphalt reflecting the light, flats with a few lit windows behind. STYLE: Raw 1990s snapshot on Fuji Superia 800 with harsh on-camera flash, flash falloff into black, heavy grain, slight motion blur. CAMERA/LIGHTING: Shot from the corner of the cage at knee height, 28mm, flash plus available light. --ar 4:5 --style raw --v 7 --no logos, text, faces looking at camera

## 4. Várzea, Brazilië

A wide colour photograph of an amateur várzea football match on a dusty red-earth pitch on the edge of São Paulo. SUBJECT: Players in mismatched faded shirts, spectators on a low concrete wall and on the roof of a parked car, a dog crossing the pitch. CONTEXT: Self-built brick houses climbing the hill behind, a hand-painted goal, power lines. STYLE: Faded colour film, Kodak Gold 200, warm dust in the air, documentary, nobody aware of the camera. CAMERA/LIGHTING: Shot from a rooftop at the side of the pitch, 35mm, late afternoon haze. --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 7 --no logos, text

## 5. Detail: oude bal en krijt

A close black and white photograph of a scuffed old leather football resting against a chalk-drawn goal on a brick wall. SUBJECT: The ball and the rough chalk lines, a kid's hand just entering the frame. CONTEXT: A narrow alley, worn bricks, a puddle. STYLE: Fine art documentary, high contrast, grain. CAMERA/LIGHTING: 50mm at f/2, hard low side light. --ar 3:2 --style raw --v 7 --no logos, text

## 6. Meisjes die voetballen

A black and white candid photograph of a girls' football game on a concrete court in an inner-city neighbourhood. SUBJECT: A girl with braids caught mid-sprint with the ball, two defenders chasing, all in mismatched training tops. Faces partly turned away, joy in the body language. CONTEXT: Graffiti on a low wall, a basketball hoop without a net, flats behind. STYLE: Raw documentary, Tri-X, grain, slight motion blur, real. CAMERA/LIGHTING: 85mm from the side of the court, panning with the runner, overcast. --ar 3:2 --style raw --v 7 --no logos, text
